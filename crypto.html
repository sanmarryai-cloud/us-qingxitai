动手前自检清单Pre-trade checklist

  • 硬止损已写好；仓位 = 账户风险% ÷ 每单位风险（不要凭感觉）Hard stop written; size from account risk% ÷ risk per unit (not gut-sized)
  • 1R 已定义；计划在约 1.5–2R 分批止盈（尤其 A 爆发）1R defined; plan for partials near 1.5–2R (especially A-burst)
  • 时间止损：2–5 天是「证明自己」窗口——不动就清，已走强可继续拿Time stop: 2–5 days is prove-it — clear if dead, may hold if already strong
  • 近突破：等突破+放量确认，且次日不快速失败再进If near-breakout: only after break + volume confirm + no quick next-day fail
  • 龙头 B：按均线/相对 BTC 结构离场，不要死磕固定涨幅If leader B: exit via MA/structure vs BTC, not a fixed % gain
  • 事件 C：仓位是今日想法里最小的一档If event C: size is the smallest sleeve among today's ideas
  • 市场体制匹配策略（突破更偏 RISK_ON / MIXED）Regime matches strategy (breakouts prefer RISK_ON/MIXED)
  • 新开仓总闸：BTC>SMA50 且 BTC近20日回撤≤15% 且 BTC ATR%≤7%（已有持仓不受影响）New-entry crypto gate: BTC>SMA50 AND BTC 20d DD≤15% AND BTC ATR%≤7% (open positions exempt)
  • 加密 24/7 交易——周末流动性与美股不同，注意跳空Crypto moves 24/7 — gaps and weekend liquidity differ from US equities
  • 不要同时堆叠多只高度相关山寨（多数山寨≈BTC贝塔）Not stacking many highly correlated alts (most alts ≈ BTC beta)
